| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![feature] | Vertical timeline columns are ordered by context parent/child tree (DFS) instead of flat alphabetical |

| ![feature] | <details><summary>Recursive collapse/expand per context in vertical timeline</summary><br>Clicking a group bar's glyph or a per-context header toggles that node's `collapsed` state. Ancestor-chain walk is cycle-guarded.</details> |
